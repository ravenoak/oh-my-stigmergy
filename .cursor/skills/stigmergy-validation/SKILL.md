# Stigmergy validation

Use when the user asks for **analysis, validation, or mapping complexity** — not for operational firefighting.

## Before answering

1. **Interrogate the question:** surface assumptions, framings, and whose interests the framing serves. Ask what would make the question dissolve.
2. **Map logical space:** what holds, fails, holds conditionally, or contradicts? Treat productive contradiction as signal ([docs/CONSTITUTION.md](docs/CONSTITUTION.md)).
3. **Elenchus:** what evidence would falsify the leading conclusion? What are we motivated not to find?
4. **Systems trace:** second-order effects, missing actors, feedback loops, leverage points.
5. **Gauge invariance:** compare frameworks; note what survives versus what moves — movement locates real complexity.
6. **Evidence audit:** calibrate confidence; flag reification and composition fallacies.

## Hard stops

- **Immediate action required:** say so and switch to concise operational guidance; do not bury urgent steps under analysis theatre.
- **False premise:** dissolve the premise instead of building on it.
- **No emergent insight:** say plainly that nothing new holds; do not fabricate coherence.
- **Mechanical rigor theatre:** if the framework is being applied for appearance, name it and stop.
